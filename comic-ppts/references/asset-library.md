# Bundled library

Resolve all paths relative to this skill folder, never to the original chat's workspace. Read assets/library-index.json for an inventory and references to exact bytes. Prefer the bundled assets over regenerating near-duplicates. Review each selected image before placement. Overview PNG labels are for selection, not final slide artwork.

| Folder under assets/library/ | Contents / use |
|---|---|
| backgrounds-light-and-mixed | 10 original backgrounds; ivory content layouts and dark title/audio scenes |
| backgrounds-dark | 10 additional dark backgrounds, numbered 11–20 |
| panels-white-full | 20 white/black comic layouts with headers, captions and callouts |
| panels-white-partial | 20 white partial-page overlays, numbered 21–40; main pane area 23–39% |
| panels-dark-on-light | Matching 20 navy pane overlays plus ready-to-use-backgrounds/ composites |
| teaching-components | 20 arrows, stars, badges, blank answer strips, footers and callouts |
| tabs-and-panes | 10 independent tabs and 10 independent panes in varied sizes |
| mouth-sonic-preferred | Male and female youthful sonic hero candidates; neither gender finalized |
| mouth-earlier-concepts | Five earlier concepts for optional exploration |
| supporting-cast | 10 villain and 10 civilian candidates on four labeled lineup sheets |

Each vector group includes PNG/SVG versions, overview, guide and JSON geometry. Use guides for intended placement; textSafe rectangles in tabs-and-panes/component-data.json give clear interior regions in source pixels. Scale them to the placed image coordinates. Do not put text over a title tab's bevel or a callout tail.

The Mouth's preferred direction combines the young Street Speaker with Sonic Knight armor and coral sonic effects. The two combined male/female options are candidate variants, not two canon heroes. Preserve whichever candidate the user selects across a deck. The Ear has a name and listening theme but no approved character design yet. Do not label an unrelated bundled character as The Ear. Use an abstract listening symbol when needed or create an explicit new design with an available image-generation or image-editing tool.

The supporting cast is a set of concepts, not finalized canon. Name labels are baked into the lineup sheets. Do not place an entire lineup as though it were a transparent individual character. For individual poses or cutouts, use an available image-generation or image-editing tool with the selected reference and preserve appearance. The existing hero PNGs include navy scenery; independently moving a hero requires a real isolated cutout. A fade of the full illustration, as in the sample, animates the illustration as a unit.
