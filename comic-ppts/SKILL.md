## Essential rules

- Comic panes organize the lesson. Vary their count, size, orientation and placement, and use partial-page panes to keep the backgrounds visible.
- Put THINK, PAIR, SHARE and other stage titles inside the header tabs. Put teaching instructions in main panes and short hints, model answers, reminders or takeaways in footer/callout boxes.
- Choose a larger tab or pane when content needs room. Keep text native and editable; prioritize classroom readability over small labels. Use Arial consistently, bold for headings.
- Prefer exact bundled artwork in assets/library/. Preserve aspect ratios and transparency; preview before placing text.
- Use the youthful sonic Mouth candidates when appropriate. Neither gender is final. The Ear's visual design has not been approved. Cast lineups and earlier hero concepts remain candidates.
- Use native short transitions and meaningful click reveals when supported. Keep slide advance manual during classroom activities. Validate the exact exported PPTX in slideshow playback when possible; static renders cannot prove motion.
- The sample's 3 slides, lesson topic and wording are examples, not required content or slide-count defaults. Its labels below header tabs are superseded by the user's instruction to place labels inside tabs.

## Start and finish

Plan purpose, pane layout, header/footer text and click sequence for each slide. Follow references/workflow.md and the available PowerPoint authoring workflow for authoring, rendering and export. When using characters, preserve the selected reference appearance and use an available image-generation or image-editing tool for new poses or transparent cutouts. Never claim an entire labeled cast sheet is an isolated character asset.

Inspect all slides at full size, correct text fit and contrast, and verify native timing after the final export. Return the PowerPoint and clearly state any motion limitation. An already validated reference is not proof that a new deck's effects work.
