# White comic panel layouts

20 widescreen layouts with white panel interiors, heavy black ink outlines, overlapping title tabs, caption boxes and speech-style callouts. Gutters are transparent so the earlier comic backgrounds can show through. All text areas are blank. PNG assets are 1920 × 1080, and SVG assets scale without losing sharpness.

Place a layout over the background and add editable PowerPoint text boxes above it. Use navy or black text. Keep body text below the title tabs; small caption boxes and callouts are intended for short labels, tips or explanations. White interiors are opaque. SVG groups can be converted to editable shapes in PowerPoint versions that support that feature.

- **01-focus**: One question, image, quotation or listening prompt.
- **02-pair-equal**: Pair discussion, compare two ideas, or speaker and listener roles.
- **03-pair-horizontal**: Before and after, claim and evidence, or listen then respond.
- **04-diagonal-duel**: Two contrasting viewpoints in an energetic debate.
- **05-feature-sidebar**: Main passage or visual beside vocabulary, questions or notes.
- **06-think-pair-share**: Three steps of think, pair, share; one step per panel.
- **07-three-beats**: Beginning, middle and end; three listening stages.
- **08-prompt-two-responses**: Shared prompt above two partners or opposing positions.
- **09-two-ideas-synthesis**: Two viewpoints above a shared conclusion or report-back.
- **10-hero-two-details**: Main image or model alongside two supporting examples.
- **11-four-square**: Four groups, four questions, or evidence and examples.
- **12-four-step-strip**: Four short stages, instructions or story moments.
- **13-debate-arena**: Motion at top, two sides in the middle, verdict at bottom.
- **14-prompt-three-groups**: One prompt above three group responses or perspectives.
- **15-jigsaw-five**: Central task beside four expert notes or supporting details.
- **16-evidence-board**: One claim or question above four evidence or response panels.
- **17-six-stations**: Six group stations, six prompts, or a short comic sequence.
- **18-six-turns**: Three rounds of dialogue between two partners.
- **19-eight-moments**: Eight short story moments, sequencing cards or quick prompts.
- **20-nine-grid**: Nine brainstorming ideas, teams or vocabulary examples.
