# Dark panes on light comic backgrounds

20 layouts matching partial-page arrangements 21–40. Dark navy panes, black outlines, coral/cyan title tabs and dark caption callouts.

- **ready-to-use-backgrounds/**: flattened 1920 × 1080 PNGs with the light ivory comic background included. Set one as the slide background, then add editable text above the panes.
- **PNG and SVG files in this folder**: transparent panel overlays. Place these at full slide size over any light background.
- **00-dark-on-light-overview.png**: all 20 combinations for browsing.

Use white or warm ivory text inside navy panes and callouts, and dark navy text in coral/cyan title tabs. Keep text below the tabs and clear of inset callouts. The assets contain no text. Main panels occupy 23–39% of the slide, leaving ample background visible.

- **21-left-feature-dark**: Main prompt at left; keep the right half open for city art or a hero.
- **22-right-feature-dark**: Main prompt at right; keep the left half open for background or a hero.
- **23-lower-story-dark**: One wide question or summary below a large open scene.
- **24-top-briefing-dark**: Brief instructions across the top with the lower scene exposed.
- **25-floating-focus-dark**: A central question surrounded by visible background.
- **26-left-pair-dark**: Two partners or before/after prompts on the left.
- **27-right-pair-dark**: Two ideas on the right with a large open area at left.
- **28-bottom-partners-dark**: Pair discussion or opposing views below an open scene.
- **29-top-partners-dark**: Two short prompts at top while preserving the lower background.
- **30-diagonal-dialogue-dark**: A dialogue moving from upper left to lower right, with the middle open.
- **31-corner-conversation-dark**: Alternating voices in opposite corners; keep the center visible.
- **32-bottom-think-pair-share-dark**: Three short stages along the bottom with the scene visible above.
- **33-left-three-steps-dark**: Three stage instructions down the left; reserve the right for imagery.
- **34-right-three-steps-dark**: Three stage instructions down the right with a large open left area.
- **35-ascending-story-dark**: Three story beats rise diagonally across a largely visible scene.
- **36-open-center-debate-dark**: Motion in a top tab, opposing arguments at the sides, open center for imagery.
- **37-four-corner-notes-dark**: Four observations frame an open central picture or background feature.
- **38-feature-and-cards-dark**: A main prompt at left with two smaller examples lower right.
- **39-l-shaped-sequence-dark**: Three prompts form an L, leaving the upper right open.
- **40-five-quick-prompts-dark**: Five short labels or discussion cues around an open central background.
