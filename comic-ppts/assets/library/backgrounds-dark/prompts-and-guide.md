# Dark comic backgrounds, set 2

Ten widescreen PNG backgrounds, numbered 11-20. Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Use white or warm ivory text in the quiet dark regions. Consistent navy/charcoal, coral and cyan comic palette. Speaking and listening symbols represent The Mouth and The Ear.

## Prompt direction for 11-14

Modern superhero comic style, bold black ink, angular panels, cel shading, edge Ben-Day halftone and paper grain. Midnight navy #071a35 and near-black dominate at least 85 percent of canvas. Cyan and coral edge accents, tiny ivory/yellow highlights. No people, text, letters, numbers or watermark. Huge quiet dark writing area; no white panels. Full bleed landscape 16:9.

### 11-midnight-rooftops

Opening scene: urban rooftop skyline restricted to lowest 18 percent, cyan rim lighting, coral glints, uninterrupted navy sky and small corner shards.

### 12-coral-speaking

Speaking prompt: near-black quiet canvas, coral ink slashes and speech-wave emblem at far right, navy edge halftones.

### 13-cyan-listening

Listening prompt: navy canvas, cyan listening arcs cropped at left edge, tiny emblem bottom left, clear right writing space.

### 14-dark-dialogue

Pair discussion: two equally sized blank navy comic panels, cyan left frame, coral right frame, slim angular charcoal gutter.
