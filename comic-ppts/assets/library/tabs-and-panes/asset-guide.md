# Comic tabs and standalone panes

10 independent title/footer tabs and 10 independent panes. Each asset comes as a transparent PNG plus a scalable SVG. Interiors are opaque white or pale tan, with black outlines and offset ink shadows. There is no baked-in text.

## Combining them in PowerPoint

Insert a pane and size it to your content. Place a tab over its top or bottom edge, bring the tab to the front, and add an editable text box over the tab. Add a separate text box in the main pane. Group each tab with its label if you want to move or animate them together.

Choose a wider or taller tab when a title needs more space. Keep titles comfortably readable, generally 24–32 pt at the size used on the slide. Allow extra room at slanted ends and around the black outline. Two-line tabs provide room for longer headings without compressing the type. Large panes work for explanations; small panes and narrow sidebars work for short prompts. The optional textSafe rectangles in component-data.json describe clear interior regions in source pixels; they contain no text objects.

Place answer text in the footer label or long footer pane and apply an entrance animation to that editable text when needed. These asset files themselves contain no animations.

## Tabs

- **01-tab-short-heading** (360 × 160): Short stage names such as Think, Pair or Share.
- **02-tab-medium-heading** (520 × 170): Headings such as Your turn or Key idea.
- **03-tab-long-heading** (740 × 180): Longer headings such as Listening challenge.
- **04-tab-wide-heading** (1000 × 190): A full activity title spanning a wide pane.
- **05-tab-page-banner** (1500 × 210): A large title tab for the top of a slide or several panes.
- **06-tab-two-line-heading** (520 × 290): Two short lines, with room for a readable stage subtitle.
- **07-tab-wide-two-line** (860 × 300): A longer two-line title or a heading plus a brief instruction.
- **08-tab-bottom-edge-tab** (640 × 190): Attach to a lower edge for a short takeaway or answer label.
- **09-tab-side-flag** (560 × 240): Place on a side edge for a prompt, hint or group label.
- **10-tab-footer-label** (900 × 260): A short answer, reminder or exit-ticket message.

## Panes

- **13-pane-medium-square** (680 × 680): A question, picture or short set of notes.
- **14-pane-tall-portrait** (620 × 980): A speaking role, longer prompt or individual task.
- **15-pane-landscape-pane** (1100 × 650): An activity prompt with enough room for examples.
- **16-pane-large-feature** (1400 × 850): The main teaching content or visual for a slide.
- **17-pane-narrow-sidebar** (430 × 1000): Vocabulary, roles, keywords or compact reminders.
- **18-pane-long-footer** (1700 × 290): A full-width answer reveal, recap or exit ticket.
- **19-pane-angled-pane** (1000 × 650): A debate viewpoint, contrasting idea or action beat.
- **20-pane-speech-pane** (1050 × 650): A character utterance, interview question or model response.
