# Comic teaching components

20 individually sized transparent PNG and SVG components matching the coral/cyan/navy comic theme. These are blank visual assets; text added in PowerPoint remains editable.

## Add and reveal an answer

Insert a strip, footer or callout. Add text boxes over its blank fields. Select the asset and its text, group them, then apply an entrance animation such as Appear with Start set to On Click. To show the empty strip first, animate only the answer text. Use navy text on white, gold, cyan or coral, and ivory text in navy fields.

All surrounding areas are transparent, including the center of the focus brackets. SVGs remain crisp when scaled. PNGs have individual canvas sizes, so insert them as ordinary pictures rather than stretching every component to fill the slide. Footer assets are designed for a 16:9 slide width.

- **01-right-arrow** — 580 × 370: Point to an example, answer or next stage.
- **02-left-arrow** — 580 × 370: Connect a note to content on its left.
- **03-turn-arrow** — 580 × 430: Show the next step or direct attention down to a response.
- **04-two-way-arrow** — 700 × 300: Compare viewpoints or show a two-way exchange.
- **05-gold-star** — 400 × 400: Highlight a strong answer or achievement.
- **06-comic-burst** — 500 × 400: Add your own short emphasis word, score or instruction.
- **07-star-cluster** — 650 × 340: Celebrate participation or a successful activity.
- **08-check-badge** — 400 × 400: Mark a verified answer or completed task.
- **09-revisit-badge** — 400 × 400: Mark a response to revisit, revise or discuss.
- **10-step-badge** — 400 × 400: Add an editable number or short group label.
- **11-answer-strip** — 1300 × 280: Put a label in the small tab and an answer in the long blank field.
- **12-hint-tab** — 850 × 340: Add a short hint or scaffold; reveal it on click.
- **13-key-point-ribbon** — 1200 × 280: Add a takeaway, model phrase or reminder.
- **14-single-answer-footer** — 1920 × 250: An answer or summary footer; use ivory text inside the navy area.
- **15-paired-answer-footer** — 1920 × 280: Two partners, alternative answers or claim and evidence.
- **16-three-part-footer** — 1920 × 310: Three short answer fields for think–pair–share, reflection or an exit ticket.
- **17-speech-callout** — 900 × 470: Add a model utterance, quotation or feedback.
- **18-thought-callout** — 900 × 480: Add a prediction, thinking prompt or reflection.
- **19-focus-brackets** — 1000 × 600: Frame existing slide content; the entire center remains transparent.
- **20-lightning-underline** — 1200 × 180: Underline a key phrase or connect a short heading to an example.
