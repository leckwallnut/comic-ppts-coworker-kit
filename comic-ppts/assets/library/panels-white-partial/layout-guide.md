# Partial-page white comic layouts: 21-40

Main panel coverage ranges from 23% to 39% of the slide. Small tabs, outlines and shadows add slightly to that area.

Keep each overlay at full slide size to preserve its intended placement. Insert your comic background first, then the overlay, then editable text boxes or images within the white panels. Use dark navy or black text. The surrounding areas are transparent. The overview uses an earlier city background to demonstrate the exposed areas; that background is not baked into the assets. Titles shown in the overview are not on the individual assets.
